Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Animations Studio is a private tool used only by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own social media accounts. It has no public users and does not collect data from viewers.

What we store

  • For each connected account (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook): the account ID, display name and username, and the access and refresh tokens the platform issues. Tokens are stored encrypted.
  • For the owner's own posts: post ID, title or caption, link, thumbnail link, publish time and public counts (views, likes, comments, shares), so the owner can follow how the videos do.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or process data about other TikTok users or viewers.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.

Where it is kept

All data stays in the app's own database on the owner's computer. It is sent only to the platform it belongs to, through that platform's official API.

Retention and deletion

Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its tokens immediately. Access can also be revoked in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. Synced post data is deleted together with the account.
