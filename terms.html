Only the owner signs in to the app. The app connects to a TikTok account only after the account holder signs in with TikTok and grants permission. Every post is reviewed and approved manually before it is sent.

All videos published through the app are original content created by the owner. The owner is responsible for the content and for following the terms and community guidelines of each platform, including TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. AI-generated content is labelled as such.

3. Platform access

The app uses official platform APIs (such as TikTok Login Kit, Content Posting API and Display API) only to publish the owner's videos and to read back the owner's own posts and their counts. Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services, or by disconnecting the account in the app.
